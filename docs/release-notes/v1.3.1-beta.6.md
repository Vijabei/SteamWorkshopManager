## Bug fixes

- **The app seemed frozen after a run** (#10). When the app was in the background at the end of a run - say, while you were in your browser - the "finished" message opened hidden behind the other window, without a taskbar button, and locked the main window until it was found. It now comes up with the main window, and the app's taskbar button flashes until you bring it to the front. Thanks to the logs from #1, this was found quickly: the runs themselves had all finished. The *Log* tab also no longer stops after 32,767 characters.
- **Progress text cut off, main button stretched across the window** (#11). The status text ("Installed 12 of 36 mods") now fills the row left of the buttons. *Install Mods* / *Archive Mods* and *Cancel* keep a fixed size on the right; the progress bar runs across the whole width below.
- **List buttons out of place after minimizing** (#5, regression in beta.5). After minimizing and restoring the window, the row of list buttons could sit far too high and cover the column headers. It now keeps its place.
- **Settings did not say which mode they apply to** (#12). Every tab now says so at the top - *Downloading*, *Appearance* and *Mod library* apply to both modes, *Archive mode* to archive mode only. The *Mod library* tab no longer has an export button filling most of the tab.
